## HIVE product imagery

For supplementary on-model photos, use a different Vietnamese male model, camera angle/framing, and setting for **every product and colourway across the entire catalogue**, unless the user specifies otherwise. Check and update `tasks/lookbook-register.md` so future shots do not repeat a previous model or scene. Do not make another colourway by recolouring an existing model photo. Use each colourway's product packshot as the garment reference and keep the cut, print, stitching, and fabric colour accurate. Keep packshots separate from lookbook images.

For all future on-model product images, cast a Vietnamese male model at least 1.75 m tall with a naturally tall, moderately slim build. Avoid stocky, bulky, or exaggeratedly muscular proportions. Use full-body or near-full-body framing when possible so the silhouette reads clearly. Treat generated height as a casting brief and visual direction, not a measurable fact about an AI-generated person.
